  - nothing new made for every corner, every frame: memory made per frame with the worm 642 KB -> 95 KB (the game's clean-ups, the stutters, far rarer)

  - (host, testing log) worm segments written to the log only while spiked - a hundred segments every tick were thousands of lines a second
- With friends: a move drawn at the one it really goes at - the server now tells, with its poses, whom the move set off at (Klaus' bite went on at you while a friend's blow had turned him to them: drawn on the friend). Not yet seen in a game
- Several Great Depths Worms at once no longer stall the game every few seconds (the game clearing away what the mod made: half a second at a time):
  - each shape worked out once for how it's turned and laid wherever it is - not made over for every segment that moved a little: worked out 15050 times in a test fight, now 34
  - the holes the worm comes out of kept as they're made - not searched for round every segment, every frame
  - nothing new made per thing on the ground per frame
