[h1]True Range (Client)[/h1]

The client version of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3817047528]True Range[/url] - for servers that don't have it. Only you need it.

See where an attack will land, before it lands. When a boss or monster winds up, the real area its move will hit is drawn on the ground under you - the same move shapes as True Range, worked out from the monster's own game code.

[h2]Client or server version?[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hosting, or your server has True Range:[/b] use [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3817047528]True Range[/url] (the server mod). It's the exact one.
[*][b]Public servers without it:[/b] use this one. If you join a server that has True Range, this one steps aside by itself.
[/list]

[h2]What's different from the server version[/h2]
Some things only the server knows, so here they're worked out from what your game sees:
[list]
[*]Warnings show a moment late with high ping
[*]Where a boss aims its leap or charge, and how far a charge goes, are a little less exact
[*]No cooldown flashes (e.g. Klaus' bite)
[/list]

[h2]What you see[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Faint red[/b] - later, [b]Red[/b] - soon, [b]Yellow[/b] - about to strike, [b]Deep red[/b] - striking now, [b]Purple[/b] - only freezes or puts you to sleep
[*]Projectiles: where they will land, while still in the air
[*]Things that hurt you just by being close: tentacles, bombs, spores and more
[*][b]Green[/b] - your weapon reach, [b]yellow outline[/b] - where a monster starts attacking you, [b]body ring[/b] for you and your friends
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Press [b]O[/b] in the game to open the settings window.

[h2]Bugs and feedback[/h2]
Found a bug, or an attack that doesn't match what's drawn? Tell me in the comments - the monster and the move is all I need.
