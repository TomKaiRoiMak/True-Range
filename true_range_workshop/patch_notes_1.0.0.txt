1.0.0 - out of beta

True Range is now a full release. Thanks to everyone who tested the beta.

- Attack warnings for the bosses and most monsters of the base game and its events: the real area each move will hit, drawn as it winds up, worked out from the monster's own game code
- Projectiles drawn where they will land while still in the air; things that hurt you by being close get a ring
- Your weapon reach (green), the yellow ring where a monster starts attacking you, and body rings for you and your friends
- Settings window in the game (O): change everything without leaving, saved on your computer
- All testing tools from the beta are switched off - no extra logging, no test keys
