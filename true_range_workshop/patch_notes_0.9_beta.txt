0.9 beta - first public test

- Boss attack warnings: when a boss starts a move, the real area it will hit is drawn on the ground - worked out ahead of time by running each boss's own game code, not guessed
- Colours: faint red = coming later, red = coming soon, yellow = about to strike, deep red = striking now
- Follows the fight: aims, leaps and charges move with you as the boss tracks you, and stop where the boss really stops
- Things bosses leave behind are drawn too: ice spikes, falling junk, lasers, crystals, spell circles
- Purple shapes: attacks that only freeze or put you to sleep
- Rings for every monster: red where its attack lands on you, yellow where it starts attacking
- Your own reach (green) and your body ring (orange while you stand in danger, red when hit)
- R shows every ring near you; everything else is in the mod's settings
- Works with Uncompromising Mode and Tropical Experience monsters
- Server mod: players who join get it automatically

Known: a few moves are still a little off at their edges (the Twins' charges, the Scion's dash). Please report anything that hits you outside the red, or misses you inside it.
