[h1]True Range[/h1]

See exactly where an attack will land - before it lands.

When a boss or monster winds up, True Range draws the real area its move will hit on the ground under you. Nothing is guessed: every move was worked out from the monster's own game code, so the warning follows the same rules the game does - where it aims, how far it leaps or charges, and where it stops.

[h2]What you see[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Faint red[/b] - part of the move that comes later
[*][b]Red[/b] - coming soon
[*][b]Yellow[/b] - about to strike
[*][b]Deep red[/b] - striking now
[*][b]Purple[/b] - only freezes or puts you to sleep
[*]Projectiles: where they will land, while still in the air
[*]What monsters leave behind: ice spikes, falling junk, lasers, crystals, spell circles, fire
[*]Things that hurt you just by being close: tentacles, bombs, spores and more
[/list]

[h2]Rings around you and your friends[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Green[/b] - how far your weapon reaches
[*][b]Yellow outline[/b] - how close a monster gets before it starts attacking you
[*][b]Body ring[/b] - orange while you stand where an attack can hit you, red when you're hit
[*]Friends get a body ring too, and their weapon reach in light blue if you want it
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Press [b]O[/b] in the game to open the settings window. Changes take effect at once and are saved on your computer.
[list]
[*]Which monsters are shown: the one under your mouse, the ones after you, or all nearby
[*]Your reach, your body ring, friends' body rings and reach, the yellow ring
[*]The window's key
[/list]
