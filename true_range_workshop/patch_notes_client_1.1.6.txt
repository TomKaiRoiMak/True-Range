1.1.6

New
- Settings: opacity for each part (attacks, outlines, your rings, other rings), 0% to 100% in 10% steps
- Ctrl + the settings key (O): turn everything off or on at once
- The cooldown glow round bosses taken out - less on screen
- Rings that aren't attacks (body, reach, friends, monsters' bodies, yellow ring, Abigail) can be drawn dotted, with how close the dots
- Your body ring stays white - no more red flash on a hit or orange when an attack reaches you
- "In a fight" stays on 10 s after the last sign of a fight, like the game's fight music - no more blinking off (Eye of Terror)
- Now named True Range+, with a new icon
- The bosses from the October 23 update: Hound Batilisk, Rock Master, Shrouded Queen, Grim Harrier, Umbral Abomination, Blighted Great Depths Worm (and their Blighted forms)
- Uncompromising Mode: Shadow Wixie, mindweaver, fuelseeker, haunt, Moon Tentacle mines, lightning strikes, merm counter combo, lavae's burning slime
- Tropical Experience: big foot, jellyfish, water rook, spiky turtles, twisters, palm treeguard coconuts
- Game updates: Hallowed Nights pigs, merms and bunnymen; moon pigs; Grim Harrier's runners

Playing on a server (caves, or joining a friend)
- Lag Compensation: charges and swipes aim where the server has you - no more snapping back as they set off
- Fast things (vines, shots, lavae, mini eyes, missiles) drawn where they really are, not a moment behind
- Quick throws and shots (Spat's phlegm, spitter spiders, blow darts, Wixie's volley) drawn before they strike, not after

Fixed
- Lighter drawing: rings use only as many pieces as their size needs - smoother with many monsters
- Missing pieces of shapes from 1.1.5
- Thrown things: where they land no longer slides or jumps as they fly
- Charges (Eye of Terror, Twins, Clockwork Rook, knights): drawn the way they really go
- Klaus: bite and leaping chomp drawn where they really land
- Shrouded Queen: her swipe drawn as the three lanes her shadows run; no warning behind her
- Celestial Champion: tri-beam side beams aimed right; spike trails stay drawn until they come up
- Umbral Abomination: teleport and optic blast drawn where they really are
- Bite attacks (pigs, merms, Rock Lobsters, bats...) drawn only in front, as they hit
- Bee Queen: no lanes for guards diving from the sky
- A crash when dozens of shots appear at once
