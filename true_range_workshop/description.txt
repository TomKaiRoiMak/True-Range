[h1]True Range[/h1]

[b]See exactly where an attack will land - before it lands.[/b]

When a boss or monster winds up, True Range draws the real area its move will hit, right on the ground. Nothing is guessed: every move was worked out from the monster's own game code - where it aims, how far it leaps or charges, where it stops, and when it stops following you. Step out of the red and you're out of the hit.

[h2]Reading the colours[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Faint red[/b] - part of the move that comes later
[*][b]Red[/b] - coming soon
[*][b]Yellow[/b] - about to strike: move now
[*][b]Deep red[/b] - striking now
[*][b]Purple[/b] - only freezes or puts you to sleep
[*][b]Pale[/b] - how far a monster reaches, for those that strike anywhere in range
[/list]

[h2]What gets a warning[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Every move of every boss and monster[/b] of the base game and its events, up to the latest rifts
[*][b]Shots[/b] - drawn as a lane, or where they'll land, only when they're coming at you
[*][b]What monsters leave behind[/b] - ice spikes, falling junk, lasers, crystals, sinkholes, spell circles, fire
[*][b]Things that hurt you just by being close[/b] - tentacles, bombs, spores and more
[*][b]Leaps and charges[/b] - drawn where they really land
[/list]

[h2]Mod support[/h2]
Turned on by itself when the server runs these mods - nothing to set up.
[list]
[*][b]Uncompromising Mode[/b] (the regular version and the beta, each from its own code) - its new monsters and bosses (Hooded Widow, Wilting and Moonmaw Dragonfly, Clockwork Pawns, Automaton Abomination, its hounds and more) and every move it adds to the game's own (Deerclops' mutations, Bearger's sinkholes, Moose's super hop...)
[*][b]Tropical Experience[/b] - the monsters and bosses of Shipwrecked, Hamlet and the Forge (Tiger Shark, Dragoon, Pugalisk, Ancient Hulk, Infernal Swineclops, Boarrior, the Kraken...)
[/list]
Both are worked out from the mods' own code, the same way as the base game.

[h2]Rings around you and your friends[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Green[/b] - how far your weapon reaches
[*][b]Yellow outline[/b] - how close a monster gets before it starts attacking you
[*][b]Body ring[/b] - orange while an attack can hit you where you stand, red when you're hit
[*][b]Friends[/b] - a body ring each, and their reach in light blue if you want it
[*][b]Abigail[/b] - her reach while she's fighting (or always, or never); her commands show where she'll act as you aim them
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Press [b]O[/b] in the game. Changes take effect at once and are saved on your computer.
[list]
[*]Which monsters are shown: the one under your mouse, the ones after you, or all nearby
[*]Your reach, your body ring, the yellow ring, Abigail's reach
[*]Friends' body rings and reach
[*]The window's key
[/list]
